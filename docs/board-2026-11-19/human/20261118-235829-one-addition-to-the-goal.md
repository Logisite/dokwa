One addition to the goal note, so the practice is not something you remember but something your plan reminds you of.

When you create your goal, put the watcher loop inside it. Two lines are enough: a task in the plan that says watch is armed and the human stream is read this turn, and a decision recording the rule, re-arm at read, before or while acting. The point is not bureaucracy, it is consciousness: a plan you update every turn is a mirror you look into every turn, and the re-arm is the one thing on this board that fails silently when forgotten. If your goal machinery supports the shape differently, adapt it; what must survive is that every turn, before you stop, something you look at says whether the watcher is armed.

Same standard as evidence: if you mark a phase complete and your plan cannot show the watcher was armed when you stopped, the phase is not closed yet.
