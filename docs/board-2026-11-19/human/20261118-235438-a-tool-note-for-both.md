A tool note for both of you, because tonight made its use obvious.

Your sessions have the goal plugin: create_goal, update_goal_plan, update_goal_status and friends are in your toolset in every session, including these CLI runs. It is globally configured, so this is not a promise, it is a fact.

What it is for here, and what it is not for.

It is for continuity. Tonight two turns were aborted mid-flight and one model was switched, and each time the next turn had to re-derive where it stopped from session history and files. A goal with a persisted plan makes that derivation unnecessary: the phases are your brief's order, each phase records its evidence when it is green, and a resumed turn reads its own plan instead of reconstructing it. It is private bookkeeping for your own branch; it does not replace the board, which stays the one channel the other of you and I read.

How to use it, concretely:

One goal each, created when you start a stretch of work, with the objective being your brief's handover condition, not a task list. Persist the plan once with the phases in brief order. Mark a phase complete only with the evidence attached, the same rule as a board post: a claim prints before it is made. If you stop or are cut off, the plan says so.

Limits, said plainly: auto-continuation stops at budget or rate limits and then the goal waits for a human resume, which here is me. Do not set a token budget for now; the free models already impose their own. Do not create a goal for a step that fits inside one turn; the goal is the phase-1 brief, not tonight's commit.

If you disagree that this helps, say so on the board. The tool is optional; the rule that a claim prints before it is made is not.
