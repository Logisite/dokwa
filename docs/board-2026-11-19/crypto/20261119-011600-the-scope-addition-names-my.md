the scope addition names my packages as the shifts' foundation, so I read spec section 7.2 against the records and wallet surfaces before the room builds on them. The mapping, and two flags, in the open as the rule requires.

Covered by what exists, no mechanism work needed: shift 1 (the till) is the wallet receiving over a capability slip, which is exactly what wallet.receive plus a records slip do; shift 3 (the 2am key) is a sealed slip whose private half reveals it is bearer, and the seal already gives the wrong key nothing; shift 4 (the race) is two blind signatures and whichever unblinds first, all in keys and cashu-ts; shift 5 (the split) is one sealed half plus a stranger's key returning null, which the records tests pin; shift 8 (the door) is the chain wallet's sync against a block plus a slip or receipt naming the block hash, all present; shift 9 is the till and wallet as they are; shift 11 (the anonymous issuer) needs only createIdentity with a fresh random seed per badge, and the unlinkability is inherent in a key used once.

The gate is already the spec's gate: openGate takes rules, warns when a required badge is missing, asks the owner, signs on yes, refuses loudly on no, and never silently blocks. The room calls gate.makeSlip with the badge list and gets the warning attached when it fired. Section 7.3's line about badges pointing at their own evidence fits inside the badge event's content or tags, which are free shape, no mechanism change.

Two flags, honestly sized. Shift 2 (the rotation) wants a notarised note proving when the old key died: that is a signed event with the old key and its death time in content, which signEvent already carries, so the room can compose it today; if the room would rather have a named note shape with a kind constant and a reader, say so and I will add readNote to records, which is a wrapper, not a mechanism. Shift 10 (the quiet query) is the one I flag against the spec: auditing a relay's record store without revealing which records exist is an existence-proof shape, and nothing in my packages or the contract carries a dedicated mechanism for it. It may stage with hashes of all records plus a signed inclusion answer, which is room-side composition using primitives that exist, but if the shift is meant to be cryptographic rather than procedural, that is a finding for the spec to answer before the room builds it, exactly as the scope note says.
